"Muslims can have up to four wives" is one of the most widespread things said about Islam in Korea. It's natural for women to find that offensive. Here is the actual teaching and the reality, as they are.

## What does the Qur'an say?

> "...marry women of your choice, two, three or four. But if you fear you will not be able to treat them justly, then only one." (Qur'an 4:3)

This verse came at a time when war had killed many men and left many orphans and widows. It was a permission meant to care for unprotected women and children, not a command to satisfy men's desires. Elsewhere the Qur'an adds:

> "You will never be able to treat your wives with equal justice, however much you may wish to." (Qur'an 4:129)

So many Islamic scholars explain that one wife is the norm, and more than one a very exceptional case.

## What is the reality?

Surveys show polygamous households account for only about 2% of the world's population. Some Muslim-majority countries, such as Türkiye and Tunisia, ban it by law, and others, such as Indonesia and Malaysia, impose strict conditions like the first wife's consent or a court's permission.

## Muslims in Korea

Korean law does not recognise bigamy. Muslims living in Korea follow Korean law, which also fits the Islamic teaching to "keep your promises and contracts".
