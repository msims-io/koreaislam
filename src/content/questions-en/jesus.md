If you're a Christian, this is probably the question you most want answered. Many people have heard that "Muslims deny Jesus", but in fact Muslims love and revere Jesus deeply.

## Jesus in the Qur'an

In the Qur'an, Jesus is called *Isa* in Arabic, and his name appears 25 times. The Qur'an says of Jesus:

- **Virgin birth**: an angel appeared to Mary and announced the birth of a son, and Mary gave birth to Jesus without having known a man. (3:45-47, 19:16-22)
- **Messiah**: the Qur'an calls Jesus *al-Masih*, the Messiah. (3:45)
- **Miracles**: by God's permission he healed the blind and lepers, and raised the dead. (3:49, 5:110)
- **Word of God**: Jesus is called "a word from God". (3:45)
- **Return**: Muslims believe Jesus will come again before the Last Day.

When Muslims say Jesus's name, they add "peace be upon him". You cannot be a Muslim without believing in Jesus.

## What's different?

Let's be honest about the differences too.

- **Divinity**: Muslims believe Jesus was a human being and a prophet, not God, because God "neither begets nor is begotten" (112).
- **The cross**: the Qur'an says Jesus did not die on the cross, and that God raised him up to Himself (4:157-158).
- **Salvation**: Islam has no concept of original sin. People are born pure, and are saved through God's mercy together with their own faith and good deeds.

We know these differences matter enormously to Christians. Muslims aren't trying to belittle Christianity, only to explain what they believe. We leave the judgement to the reader.

## What we share

Jesus's teaching, "Love God and love your neighbour", is precious to Muslims too. Christians and Muslims believe in the same God of Abraham and honour the same prophets; they are like cousins in faith.
