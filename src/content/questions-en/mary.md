Catholic readers may be surprised: Mary holds a very special place in the Qur'an.

## Mary in the Qur'an

Several women appear in the Qur'an, but Mary is the only one named. She is called *Maryam* in Arabic, and her name is said to appear more often than in the New Testament.

> "The angels said: O Mary, God has chosen you and purified you, and chosen you above all the women of the worlds." (Qur'an 3:42)

Chapter 19, "Maryam", describes beautifully and tenderly how Mary gave birth to Jesus alone. The passage where Mary, in labour, leans against a palm tree and God comforts her with a stream and ripe dates is one many Muslims love (19:23-26).

## A model for Muslim women

For Muslims, Mary is a model of deep faith, purity and patience. Muslim families very often name their daughters Mariam.

The way Catholics venerate Mary and the way Muslims honour her are alike in spirit. The difference is that Muslims do not pray to Mary; they pray only to God.
