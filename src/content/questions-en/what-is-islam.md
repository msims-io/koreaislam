Many people first come across Islam in the news, so they think of it as a strange religion from a faraway country, or even a frightening one. That reaction is natural. This page explains calmly, starting from the basics, what Muslims actually believe.

## The words "Islam" and "Muslim"

**Islam** is Arabic for "submission" or "entrusting oneself". It comes from the same root as *salam*, "peace". A **Muslim** is "a person who has entrusted themselves to God", in other words someone who follows Islam.

## Six things Muslims believe

1. **One God**: God is one, and nothing is like Him. (Qur'an 112)
2. **Angels**: there are beings who carry God's word.
3. **Scriptures**: the Law of Moses (Torah), the Psalms of David, the Gospel of Jesus and the Qur'an are all revelations from God.
4. **Prophets**: God sent many prophets, from Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses and Jesus through to Muhammad (pbuh).
5. **The Day of Judgement**: when this life ends, everyone will answer for what they did.
6. **God's will (divine decree)**: everything happens within God's knowledge and will.

## Five things Muslims practise

- **Declaration of faith** (shahada): "There is no god but God, and Muhammad is the messenger of God."
- **Prayer five times a day** (salah)
- **Giving** (zakat): each year, a portion of one's wealth (usually 2.5%) goes to people in need.
- **Fasting in Ramadan** (sawm): for one month, no food or drink while the sun is up.
- **Pilgrimage** (hajj): those who can afford it go to Mecca once in their lifetime.

## Not a new religion, but the same message continued

Muslims don't see Islam as a new religion that Muhammad (pbuh) invented. They believe it is the last renewal of the same message that Abraham, Moses and Jesus brought: serve the one God and live a good life. As the Qur'an says:

> "We believe in God and in what was sent down to us, and what was sent down to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and their descendants, and what was given to Moses and Jesus. We make no distinction between any of them." (Qur'an 2:136)

## Who are Muslims?

Muslims are not only Arabs. The country with the most Muslims is Indonesia, followed by Pakistan, India and Bangladesh. Arabs make up only about 20% of all Muslims. In Korea, Korean Muslims live alongside Muslims who have come from many countries.
